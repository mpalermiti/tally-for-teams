Tally for Teams

Live Microsoft Teams controls. A lit key means it's live.

Download View on GitHub

Free and open source · macOS 13+ · Stream Deck 7.1+ · the new Teams desktop app · full state in English Teams

Why “Tally”? On a TV set, the tally light is the small red lamp on a camera that tells everyone it's live. Tally puts that light on your Stream Deck: a lit key means that thing is live — your mic, your camera, your raised hand, your shared screen.

Made for meetings.

See it at a glance.

Mic, camera, hand, and sharing at a glance. Red dot means recording. Tally doesn't guess.

Push to talk.

Tap Mute to toggle. Hold it to talk while muted, or to cough while live.

Stays out of your way.

Works with Teams in the background and never pulls it to the front.

No accidental hang‑ups.

Turn on Hold to leave and a tap can't end your meeting.

One-click setup.

Stream Deck offers a ready-made Tally profile when you install.

Private by design.

Runs entirely on your Mac. No account, no cloud. It reads only Teams' meeting buttons.

Always in step with Teams.

Press a key and Teams follows. Change something in Teams and the key follows.

Ten keys. One rule.

Amber means it's live. Red dots on Mic and Camera mean the meeting is recorded or transcribed. Keys stay neutral when Tally can't read a state.

Set up in a minute.

  1. Download Tally and double-click it.
  2. Accept the bundled Tally profile, or drag the keys you want from Tally for Teams.
  3. Press any Tally key, then allow Stream Deck in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

Optional: switch to the Tally profile during meetings from any Tally key's settings. For Leave, consider turning on Hold to leave.

Stays on your Mac.

Tally runs entirely on your Mac. It reads Teams' meeting buttons the way a screen reader does, and never messages, chat or window titles. No account, no cloud, and this page doesn't track you either.
